================================================================================
THE CORE IDEA
================================================================================

Transform x402scan from a passive transaction explorer into an active trust
verification platform by adding JOLT-Atlas zkML proof badges to every
transaction.

BEFORE:
  Transaction list shows: hash, from, to, amount

AFTER:
  Transaction list shows: hash, [✅ zkML Verified (95%)], from, to, amount
  Click badge → See full proof details (AI decision, confidence, gas cost)

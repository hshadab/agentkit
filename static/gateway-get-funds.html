How Gateway Balance Works

Your Gateway spendable balance increases when:

  • Someone sends you USDC through Gateway transfers
  • You receive minted USDC from cross-chain Gateway transfers
  • Circle credits your account (for testing purposes)

The balance decreases when you initiate Gateway transfers to other addresses or chains.
